The top-agent playbook, built in

Often enough to be remembered.
Rare enough to be welcome.

The best agents stay in touch with steady, personal follow-up that never feels like marketing. Few have the hours to keep it up. StaySold runs that playbook for you, paced for each client, so you stay top of mind without wearing out your welcome.

Small and independent offices

Compete with the big teams.

Get the follow-up system of a large brokerage without the staff to run it. Campaigns are written, scheduled and paced for you. One review a month keeps every client warm.

Large offices and teams

Automate what your best agents do by hand.

Give every agent the same proven follow-up, in their own voice. Assign each client to the right agent, and when a client writes, phone alerts reach the whole team so someone always answers in minutes. Team reports keep the results visible.

  • Paced per clientNever more than one automated message a week to any client, across text and email.
  • Personal, not promotionalMessages draw on what you know: the house, the kids, the dog, their plans.
  • Worth openingHome tips, events and trusted local pros. Things clients keep, not ads they delete.
  • Quiet modeClients who stop opening slow to a personal touch each quarter instead of getting more.
  • Easy to step backReply LESS for fewer messages or STOP to end them. Texts wait for a good hour.
