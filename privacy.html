Privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026

StaySold is a product of Alpine Consulting LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("we" or "us"). This policy explains what we collect through this website and the StaySold pilot request form, how we use it, and the choices you have.

It covers this website and pilot requests only. The StaySold service will have its own terms and privacy policy, which agents receive before they sign up.

What we collect

  • Pilot request form: your first and last name, phone number, email address, brokerage, the size of your past client and sphere list, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.
  • Emails you send us: your email address and whatever you include in the message.
  • Technical data: when you visit, your browser sends standard information such as your IP address and browser type to the services that host this site, its fonts and the form.

This site does not run advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To respond to your pilot request and contact you about it.
  • To send you text messages, only if you checked a box agreeing to them on the form.
  • To improve StaySold and plan the pilot.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect our rights.

Text messages

If you agree to texts about your pilot request, we send messages about that request. If you also agree to marketing texts, we may send product updates and offers from StaySold. Agreeing is never a condition of joining the pilot.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to stop texts at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • We do not share your mobile number or text consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Text opt-in data is not sold or shared, except with the service providers that deliver our messages.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run StaySold, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • GoHighLevel (LeadConnector), which hosts the pilot form and stores requests, and sends our texts and emails.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts on this site.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. The embedded pilot form is provided by GoHighLevel and may use cookies or similar storage so the form works.

How long we keep it

We keep pilot requests as long as we need them to talk with you about StaySold, and then delete them, unless you become a customer or the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

StaySold is for real estate professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
